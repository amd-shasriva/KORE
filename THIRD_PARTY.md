# Third-party material and attribution

KORE is proprietary and AMD-internal (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). It nevertheless incorporates,
derives from, or measures against third-party material. This file records what that material is,
under what terms it was obtained, and — importantly — **which items are not yet resolved**.

Machine-readable provenance lives in `data/release/meta/source_metadata.json`
(schema `1.0`, 28 source entries and 2 dataset entries). This document is the human-readable
companion and is authoritative for the *unresolved* list.

## 2. Source repositories used to build the midtrain corpus

27 external repositories plus this repository. Commits are pinned in `source_metadata.json`.

**Apache-2.0** — FlagGems (FlagOpen), TransformerEngine (NVIDIA), unsloth (unslothai), vllm (vllm-project)

**MIT** — GEAK (AMD-AGI), aiter (ROCm), composable_kernel (ROCm), kernels (triton-lang), rocPRIM (ROCm), triton (triton-lang)

**BSD-2-Clause** — Liger-Kernel (linkedin)

**BSD-3-Clause** — flash-attention (Dao-AILab), xformers (facebookresearch)

**Licence recorded as `SEE-REPO` — UNRESOLVED (14 repositories)** — HIP, MIOpen, Tensile, rccl,
rocFFT, rocRAND, rocSOLVER, rocSPARSE, rocThrust, rocWMMA, hipCUB (all ROCm), cutlass (NVIDIA),
pytorch (pytorch), ROCmKernelWiki (jhinpan).

`SEE-REPO` means the ingestion pipeline recorded the repository and commit but did **not** resolve
an SPDX identifier. Half the corpus sources are in this state. Each must be resolved to a concrete
licence, and its redistribution and attribution obligations checked, before any external release of
the corpus or of a model trained on it.

## 4. General-replay slice — UNRESOLVED, and material

21,486 midtrain chunks (**25.0% of the corpus**) are stamped:

```
repository_url: development-bundled://kore/general-replay
commit:         development-bundled
license:        DEVELOPMENT-INTERNAL
```

These are placeholders. The rows contain 21,447 *distinct* real instruction-tuning texts, so the
content is genuine upstream data, but the recorded provenance does not identify which upstream
dataset or revision it came from. The candidate sources the loader is written against are
OpenCodeInstruct / Magicoder, OpenThoughts3 / OpenMathInstruct-2, `tulu-3-sft-mixture`, and
ToolACE / xLAM — each with its own licence and, in some cases, restrictions on commercial or
derived use.

**The true upstream terms for a quarter of the midtrain corpus are currently unrecoverable from the
artifact.** This must be resolved by re-deriving that slice with real provenance before any external
release. It does not affect internal use.

## 5. Kernel-curriculum slice — UNRESOLVED

9,956 midtrain chunks (11.6%) carry `license: none` and **no `source_metadata` at all**. They were
appended post-hoc by `data/release/generators/augment_midtrain.py`, bypassing the corpus contract,
so they also skipped decontamination and dedup. The content is teacher-generated prose (see §6).

## 6. Teacher-generated content

Portions of the SFT, DPO, and curriculum data were generated by **Anthropic Claude** (recorded as
`claude-opus-4.8` / `claude-opus-5`) accessed through AMD's internal LLM gateway. Any external use
of teacher-generated text is subject to the applicable Anthropic terms and to AMD's gateway terms.
The Tier-1 kernel-math subset (8,873 rows) is additionally auto-verified against a deterministic
solver, but verification does not change its provenance.

## 7. Evaluation benchmarks

The retention suite evaluates against MMLU, HumanEval, LiveCodeBench, IFEval, BFCL, and MT-Bench.
Each carries its own licence and citation requirements. The frozen decontamination artifact
`data/release/meta/benchmark_artifact.json.gz` contains hashed benchmark records used to exclude
contamination; it is derived from those benchmarks and inherits their terms.

## 8. Runtime dependencies

Pinned in `requirements-conductor.txt` and `.github/constraints-ci.txt`. Principal components:
PyTorch (ROCm build), pytorch-triton-rocm, Transformers, TRL, PEFT, Accelerate, Datasets, NumPy,
scikit-learn, XGBoost, and the ROCm stack including AITER, hipBLASLt, and rocprofv3. Each is used
under its own licence; none is vendored into this repository except as noted in §2.

## Unresolved items blocking external release

1. Qwen3-14B licence and derived-checkpoint redistribution terms (§1).
2. SPDX resolution for the 14 `SEE-REPO` repositories (§2).
3. True upstream provenance and licence for the 21,486-row general-replay slice (§4).
4. Provenance and licence stamping for the 9,956-row kernel-curriculum slice (§5).
5. Anthropic and AMD-gateway terms for teacher-generated content (§6).
6. Benchmark licences and citation requirements (§7).
7. Selection of an outbound licence by an authorized AMD owner (`LICENSE`).

Until every item above is closed, this repository, its datasets, and any model trained from them
remain internal-only.
